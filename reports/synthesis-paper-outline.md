# Lead paper: *Discrete Grounding and Spectral Valuation in Dictionary Graphs*

## Working abstract (~200 words)

> A dictionary's definition digraph — an arc from each word to the words used in its gloss — carries two non-equivalent notions of "foundationalness." The **combinatorial** one is a minimum feedback vertex set: the words you must fix from outside so that every other definition unfolds acyclically (Massé's grounding-set theorem). The **spectral** one is a self-consistent eigenvector valuation — Perron–Frobenius / reverse-PageRank — that ranks a word by how productively it occurs in other definitions. We compute both on Open English WordNet (`oewn:2024`; 160,010 senses, 677,823 edges) and ask whether the spectral object adds anything the combinatorial one and raw degree do not. It does not: reverse-PageRank correlates with out-degree at ρ ≈ 0.99 (full graph) / 0.80 (Kernel), a degree-preserving edge-swap null recovers ≈ 0.61 of its ranking, and adding it to `log(out-degree)` raises R² by ≤ 0.01 for frequency, age-of-acquisition, and concreteness norms; gradual argumentation semantics behave the same way and with the wrong sign. The durable contributions are therefore the combinatorial layer — restated in argumentation vocabulary as a minimal-enforcement / FPT-backdoor object, with a renormalization-invariant *standard-MinSet* theorem borrowed from Sraffa's standard commodity — and a clear statement of *why* the spectral layer is empty here: the dictionary digraph is too homogeneous for the higher-order structure spectral methods exploit. We also report a sense-level rebuild that eliminates literal gloss self-loops, with caveats. Scope: the non-circular core of one curated dictionary, not the structure of English.

## Section structure

1. **Introduction.** Harnad's "Chinese/Chinese dictionary-go-round" and the well-foundedness question: how many words, and which, must be known from outside so the rest unfold by recursive definition? The graph formalization (Massé 2008; Picard 2013; Vincent-Lamarre 2014 — Kernel / Core / Satellites / MinSet, ~8–12% Kernel, ~1% MinSet, many MinSets). What is new here vs Vincent-Lamarre: (i) we run it on a *sense-level* rebuild of WordNet, not the first-meaning-per-POS collapse; (ii) we put the *combinatorial* notion of foundationalness side by side with a *spectral* one and report that they agree only where degree drives both; (iii) we restate FVS-grounding in formal-argumentation vocabulary (minimal enforcement / FPT backdoor) and add a renormalization-invariant standard-MinSet theorem. Frame the paper's spine up front: a *negative* core result, a *small* theorem, a *methods* contribution — and Harnad as a standing constraint, not a foil (a MinSet is not a set of human semantic primitives; a graph node is not a human word).

2. **The definition digraph and its anatomy.** Recall the Kernel / Core / Satellites / MinSet construction (Massé/Picard/V-L): leaf-stripping → Kernel; source SCCs → Core; rest of Kernel → Satellites; minimum FVS → MinSet (= Vincent-Lamarre's grounding-set, NP-hard, many MinSets — Fomin 2008's enumeration bound). The self-loop policy: a word appearing only in its own gloss is a forced 1-node FVS member (commit 7d12e64). The OEWN `lemma::pos` numbers, post the self-loop fix: 160,010 nodes / 677,823 edges; Kernel 18,151; Core 510; Satellites 17,641; `exact-small-greedy` seed ≈ 5,044 (≈3.15%); one giant Kernel SCC of 8,138; 0 residual cyclic SCCs after the seed; 65-layer DAG. Note this places OEWN inside the "biological" FVS regime — small FVS, large matching-driver set — which §6 returns to.

3. **FVS as minimal enforcement, and a standard MinSet.** *(The half-page restatement — labelled as such.)* Reading the digraph as an abstract argumentation framework: removing an FVS makes the framework acyclic, hence its extension unique; an FVS is therefore a *minimal enforcement set* that forces skeptical determinacy, and an *FPT backdoor* parameter (Dvořák–Pichler–Woltran). State plainly: this is Massé 2008's grounding-set ⇔ FVS theorem plus the known FPT-in-backdoor-size result, re-expressed — **no new mathematics**, a translation dictionary between "feedback vertex set" and "enforcement / backdoor set," useful as a *frame* (it imports the FPT machinery). Then the genuinely new piece, presented modestly: the **weighted standard-MinSet theorem**. Sraffa's standard commodity is the Perron eigenvector of the irreducible "basics" block of an input–output matrix — and basics/non-basics *is* an SCC / Frobenius-normal-form split. So for an irreducible weighted Kernel `K` with adjacency `A_K`, Perron vector `π`, and any positive grounding-cost `g(v)`, set `c(v) = g(v)/π_v`; a `c`-minimum FVS is invariant under scalar renormalization of `π` and is a Sraffa-style *standard* MinSet — canonical relative to `A_K` and `g`, not semantically ultimate. This turns "many MinSets, none privileged" into "a Pareto family of explicit standard-selection regimes" — a small result, stated as one.

4. **Spectral valuation, and why it is empty here.** Perron–Frobenius / PageRank as a self-consistent importance valuation; the two orientations: *forward* (`defining → defined`, authority — ranks definitional sinks: `magnificat`, `niobe`, …, the wrong words) vs *reverse* (`defined → defining`, PageRank on the transpose — its top *is* the abstract genus vocabulary: `act, degree, time, part, place, can, …`; `large` → 27/160,010, `body` → 29, `small` → 66). The deflation, in numbers: reverse-PageRank ρ with out-degree = 0.995 (full) / 0.797 (Kernel), ρ with in-degree ≈ −0.05; ρ with the FVS degree-key ≈ 0.37 (not the predicted > 0.6); a degree-preserving edge-swap null recovers ρ ≈ 0.61 of the real ranking, out-degree alone recovers ≈ 0.69 → "out-degree spectrally laundered." The ranking-semantics confirmation: every gradual semantics tried (h-categorizer, categoriser, burden, counting; both orientations) collapses to `1/(1+degree)` — ρ ≈ 0.6–0.95 with degree, best incremental R² over `log(out-degree)` ≈ +0.023 on a ≈0 baseline (concreteness, kernel/burden), and the *sign is wrong* under the attack reading (a heavily-used genus word has many attackers → low score; these rank dictionary leaves high). The psycholinguistic regression: joining SUBTLEX-US Zipf / Kuperman AoA / Brysbaert concreteness onto the graph, the structural block screens off the norms — ΔR²/ΔAUC ≤ 0.01 for every layer/membership outcome; reverse-PageRank and the FVS seed add ≤ +0.03 over `log(out-degree)`; the Vincent-Lamarre "deeper = later-acquired" effect vanishes once you control for structure. **State the limit explicitly: this is NOT "meaning is relationally complete."** The *lexicographer's confound* — dictionaries are written for learners, so concrete/early/frequent words become definers *because* readers are expected to know them, which *creates* the structural features — makes "block 2 adds nothing over block 1" equally consistent with "meaning is relational" and "the graph was built from psycholinguistic salience, so it screens it off." Same regression signature, opposite causation; the regression cannot tell which. The honest conclusion: on the bare `lemma::pos` digraph there is *no hidden continuous "soft grounding" signal beyond connectivity* — a result about the graph's homogeneity, not a result about meaning.

5. **The sense-level ingestion.** Why `lemma::pos` is the wrong granularity: one node per `lemma::pos` with the *first* gloss-bearing synset as representative conflates form / sense / identity-cluster; concrete damage — `no::n` enters as Nobelium (not the negation), 3,413 gloss self-loops (many artifacts: a gloss for `violin::n` saying "violin" means the intended sense, not literal self-definition), spelling-variant inflation. The typed model sketch: `Form → TokenOccurrence → Reading → Sense → IC` plus `Construction / Metadata / Provenance / AdmissionPolicy`, a 9-tag `Lexicality` enum, definition edges as `supports` between resolved **Sense** nodes (the honest bipolar AF/ADF), rival senses of a form `attack` each other, IC merge as a provenance-keeping belief-merge that never canonicalizes. The prototype's result and its honest caveats: the support-graph prototype (`build_sense_level_paper_wordnet_graph`) yields 212,478 sense nodes / 418,094 edges, Kernel 12,142, 0 residual cyclic SCCs, **zero literal emitted gloss self-loops** (vs 3,413) — but precisely: "no head sense linked to itself after the resolver excluded the head from its own candidate set and skipped 1,496 self-references," an adjacency fact under a skip-on-self resolver, not a validated WSD result. The Kernel shrink 18,151 → 12,142 is *partly* "we dropped the edges that would have formed cycles": 54% of gloss-token candidate matches (499,860 / 925,283) were dropped as ambiguous, so the sense graph has ≈2× fewer edges per node (1.97 vs 4.24). The lexicality classifier is a *rule pile* with a known per-class profile (macro-F1 ≈ 0.74 on a stratified gold set; wins short-token/symbol-code, loses taxa/chemicals — see §6 related work / the head-to-head); the 7-pair IC whitelist is a fixture, not a method. The load-bearing claim is narrow and falsifiable: *literal gloss self-loops are eliminable by resolving same-surface gloss occurrences away from the head sense, and the resulting sense graph still has acyclic definitional closure after a (non-exact) seed* — with an edge-budget-controlled comparison still owed.

6. **Related work.** Vincent-Lamarre et al. (the direct predecessor — first-meaning graphs over four dictionaries; we re-do it at the sense level and add the spectral-vs-combinatorial comparison); Massé 2008 (the grounding-set ⇔ FVS theorem we restate); Picard 2013 (Kernel/Core/Satellites anatomy); Levary 2012 (loops are semantic structure, not noise — the reason we report the loop ecology, not just cut it); Harnad 1990 (the standing constraint). The **FVS-control biology lineage** as the cleanest cross-domain link: Mochizuki-Saito 2010 ("informative nodes"), Fiedler-Mochizuki 2013 I & II, Zañudo-Yang-Albert 2017 — an FVS for control of *nonlinear* dynamical networks, with no contact with the dictionary-grounding literature that also uses FVS; their empirical FVS-control-set-size (1–18% of nodes for gene-regulatory nets) vs matching-driver-set-size (75–96%) split is the regime OEWN's ≈3.15% FVS vs ≈74% matching drivers sits in. Distributional semantics: **concede "meaning is largely relational"** — the project's own three probes (spectral, ranking, psycholinguistic) all say so; the difference between this work and an embedding is not capability but *directedness* (a corpus/embedding has no `must-know-X-before-Y` arrow, so it cannot even ask the well-foundedness question), *inspectability* (provenance, derivation chains, "why is this word foundational, what changes if you ground a different one"), and *the question asked*. Cite the head-to-head (lexicality classification: rules vs TF-IDF — a split, not a clean win; the rules' edge is the surface-form-bound subset and auditability).

7. **Limitations and future work.** Zero head-to-head accuracy wins over an embedding (the one head-to-head run was a tie + an auditability win); on a fair fight (WSD, definition modeling) the typed sense graph is unlikely to win on accuracy. The cross-dictionary stability test (vs Longman LDOCE's controlled defining vocabulary, ideally a non-pedagogical dictionary) — the discriminator for the lexicographer's confound; cite it as the in-progress agenda item (#5 of `reports/synthesis.md`). The admission theory is sketched, not wired (the "human Up-Goer list" is currently a lexicality-tag filter, not an admitted extension). Pin the scope: "the non-circular core of *this curated dictionary* under its sense-resolution choices," not "the structure of English," and not "the structure of meaning."

8. **Conclusion.** Dictionary graphs *locate the places where a symbolic lexicon cannot justify itself from within* — that is the durable thing. Grounding sets are feedback vertex sets; loops are structure, not just obstacles; there is no hidden continuous foundationalness score beyond connectivity on the bare digraph, which is itself a finding about the graph's homogeneity; the sense level is the right granularity and the rebuild is a step toward it. The grand claims (a MinSet is a set of semantic primitives; recursive definability is intrinsic meaning; the regression proves relational completeness) are not made.

# Subsidiary papers (one paragraph each)

## (a) The Perron–Frobenius "convergent rediscovery" history-of-science piece

The same matrix object — the dominant eigenvector of a non-negative irreducible relational matrix — was independently rediscovered, with near-empty cross-citation, as Sraffa's standard commodity (1960; the Perron eigenvector of the irreducible "basics" input–output block), Leontief's closed-model price vector, Potron's 1911–1913 work (ignored ~90 years), the Markov stationary distribution, PageRank (Brin–Page 1998) and its bibliometric/sociological ancestors (Hubbell 1965, Pinski–Narin 1976, Bonacich 1972/1987, Katz 1953, HITS), and the leading singular vectors of LSA's co-occurrence matrices — and, the paper notes wryly, the meanings project's own (failed) valuation layer is just this machinery again. The figure is the citation-edge timeline: nodes = the rediscoveries, edges = actual citations, showing the graph is almost disconnected across fields; Bidard–Erreygers–Parys's "All but one" (the economics↔Perron-Frobenius citation gap) is the anchor and the title gesture. The honest framing (from the math facet): it is "the same matrix algebra under an irreducibility/normalization problem," not "the same idea in every field" — what *flows* differs (value, probability, prestige, co-occurrence mass, definitional productivity), and the differences are the interesting part. Venue: a history/philosophy-of-science journal (*Studies in History and Philosophy of Science*, *Isis*, *Annals of Science*) or a methods/perspectives venue (*Notices of the AMS*, a perspectives slot at a network-science venue). Low-effort, standalone, no new computation required beyond the timeline.

## (b) The Yoneda/Harnad philosophy note

Yoneda's lemma determines an object up to isomorphism *within a fixed category* by its pattern of morphisms; Harnad's point is that for a cognitive agent the ambient category — the universe of available relata — is not given a priori, it must be *grounded into existence* non-symbolically. So the two are compatible by scope, not contradictory (Resolution A): grounding constructs/selects the base category, and once it is fixed, structural identity does real work inside it (a MinSet ≈ a generating set; MinSet non-uniqueness ≈ presentation non-uniqueness; Kernel/Core/Satellite ≈ graded grounding). The sharp rider (both philosophy facets, independently): a thoroughgoing structuralist who dissolves grounding in cognitive semantics owes *mathematics* the same story — Benacerraf's access problem is Harnad's question in mathematical clothing; you cannot apply structuralism asymmetrically. The note also offers a mechanization: `gunray`'s four-valued `UNDECIDED` verdict for a circular ungrounded definition (`a -< b`, `b -< a`, no facts → both `UNDECIDED`; add one grounding fact → the chain resolves to `YES`) is "recursive definability ≠ meaning" in working code — Harnad's argument as a small executable demonstrator. Caveat to keep in scope: the dictionary digraph is only an *analogy* to a category — its edges don't supply identities, composition, or functoriality. Venue: *Minds and Machines*, *Philosophy & Technology*, a philosophy-of-cognitive-science venue (*Review of Philosophy and Psychology*), or an applied-category-theory venue (*Compositionality*) if the categorical framing leads.

## (c) The NLP-empirical companion — *The Wrong Eigenvector* (only if not absorbed into the lead)

The spectral + ranking + sense-level-rebuild material, as a standalone CL paper, *if* the lead paper is trimmed to keep it focused (e.g. if the lead becomes the combinatorial/theorem paper and this becomes the empirical one). Thesis: on a definitional/dependency digraph, the naive "import PageRank as a foundationalness anchor" move silently imports the *wrong orientation* (authority PageRank ranks definitional sinks; you want the transpose), and even the right orientation is out-degree spectrally laundered (ρ ≈ 0.99; the edge-swap null recovers ≈ 0.61); gradual argumentation semantics behave identically and with the wrong sign; the psycholinguistic block screens off none of this beyond degree; and the right *granularity* for any of these analyses is the sense level, not `lemma::pos` — the sense-level rebuild eliminates literal gloss self-loops but reveals that the lemma-level Kernel was partly inflated by parsing artifacts and partly that the sense graph drops half its candidate edges as ambiguous. A "here is the negative result, here is the mechanism (graph homogeneity), here is the methodological warning (check edge polarity and node granularity before running centrality on your dependency graph)" paper. Venue: TACL or an ACL/EMNLP findings track (a negative-results-friendly slot); or fold it entirely into the lead paper's §4–§5 and don't write it separately — recommended unless the lead is overlong.
