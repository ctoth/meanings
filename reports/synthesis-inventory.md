## 0. Orientation in two sentences

The project asks "how small is the non-circular core of a dictionary?" — formalized as the feedback-vertex-set / Kernel-Core-Satellites-MinSet decomposition of a definition digraph, applied to Open English WordNet (`oewn:2024`), bounded above by Harnad's symbol-grounding limit (`README.md:3-7`, `reports/synthesis-minimal-core-to-expansion.md`). Three layers of work now sit on top of the base kernel tooling: (a) the **Swanson cross-domain pass** (5 deep-research briefs + Codex review + spectral/psycholinguistic follow-ups), (b) the **argumentation-framework reframing** (definition digraph = bipolar AF / ADF; sibling tools `../argumentation`, `../belief-set`, `../gunray`), and (c) the **ingestion redesign** (form → sense → IC → admission as a typed defeasible system; the Up-Goer human-vocabulary deliverable).

## 1. Settled facts / decisions

### 1.1 The self-loop fix (commit `7d12e64`) and its consequences
- `compute_kernel` now treats a gloss self-loop (`u → u`, a word in its own gloss) as a cycle; pre-fix those nodes were silently peeled out of the Kernel. (`reports/self-loop-fix-impact.md`; commits `7d12e64`, `9da2f92`, `e0ff1eb` regenerated paper reports.)
- **Kernel grew 12,853 → 18,151** (post-fix); Core 288 → 510; Satellites 12,565 → 17,641; Kernel SCCs ~3,841 → 9,139; source SCCs 286 → 508. (`reports/self-loop-fix-impact.md`, `reports/argumentation-bridge-oewn.md` "post-self-loop-fix" table.) `oewn:2024` graph: 160,010 nodes, 677,823 edges, 3,413 self-loops (the `self-loop-fix-impact.md` header says 3,413; `argumentation-bridge-oewn.md` reports 2,804 self-loop singletons inside the Kernel — different scopes, not a contradiction).
- The fix's *only* mechanical effect is the Kernel-size increase (the self-loop nodes plus the acyclic upstream that used to be stripped with them). (`reports/self-loop-fix-impact.md` note 1.)
- **`bounded-scc` (8-pass cap) leaves a residual cyclic SCC** on the post-fix Kernel (1 residual SCC) → `analyze_kernel` skips `compute_layer_map`, so no layer histogram for that method. `exact-cutting` goes from 1 → 11 residual SCCs post-fix. **Only `exact-small-greedy` (loops to completion) gives residual = 0 and a full layer map**, before and after the fix. (`reports/self-loop-fix-impact.md` table + note 2.) Post-fix seed sizes: `bounded-scc` 3,620; `exact-small-greedy` 5,044; `exact-cutting` 3,584.
- Decided: keep the fix (a 1-node self-loop is a forced FVS member by the textbook definition); whether OEWN gloss self-loops are *real* circularity vs parsing artifacts is the open question the ingestion redesign addresses (see §4). (`reports/swanson-synthesis.md` recommended-next item 3; `notes/upgoer-identity-clusters.md`.)

### 1.2 The spectral / Perron-Frobenius result — reverse-PageRank ≈ laundered out-degree
- Authority (forward) PageRank is the wrong object: it ranks definitional sinks (`magnificat`, `niobe`, …) at top; FVS-seed hubs (`small`, `large`, `body`) sit in the bottom decile; Core mean PageRank is *below* uniform. (`reports/spectral-valuation-oewn.md`, `reports/perron-frobenius-oewn.json`.)
- Reverse-PageRank (PageRank on the transpose) gets the *top words* right qualitatively (`act, degree, time, event, part, place, can, quality, …`; `large`→27/160010, `body`→29, `small`→66) — matches the FVS-seed top set. (`reports/spectral-valuation-oewn.md` §0-1, `reports/spectral-valuation-oewn.json`.)
- **But the quantitative prediction fails:** Spearman ρ(reverse-PageRank, FVS degree-key `internal_out+internal_in`) over the Kernel = **0.316**, not the predicted >0.6. Reason: reverse-PageRank ≈ pure out-degree (ρ 0.995 full / 0.746 Kernel; ρ with in-degree ≈ −0.05), while the FVS key sums out+in. (`reports/spectral-valuation-oewn.md` §0.)
- **Null-model verdict (decisive):** a degree-preserving edge-swap null recovers ρ ≈ 0.52 of the real reverse ranking; out-degree alone recovers ρ ≈ 0.68. *Neither the spectral object nor the FVS seed adds much over raw out-degree.* Reverse-PageRank is "out-degree spectrally laundered." (`reports/swanson-synthesis.md` §"one real result"; confirmed numerically in §1.3 below.)
- Un-damped Perron on the giant Kernel SCC localizes on technical micro-cliques (`potassium/rubidium/sodium`, `vasoconstrictor/vasopressin/angiotensin`); λ* ≈ 3.744; a degree re-skin, not foundational vocabulary. (`reports/spectral-valuation-oewn.md` §1, `reports/swanson-synthesis.md`.)

### 1.3 The psycholinguistic regression result
- Data join: `paper-wordnet` OEWN + SUBTLEX-US Zipf / Kuperman AoA / Brysbaert concreteness, keyed on lemma. All-three intersection: 27,356 nodes (17.1% overall, 53.7% inside the Kernel). (`reports/psycholinguistic-regression-findings.md` §1.) **NB: these regressions were run against the *pre-self-loop-fix* Kernel** — the findings report quotes Kernel 12,853 / Core 288 / seed 2,370 / 65-layer DAG (`exact-small-greedy`). The qualitative verdicts are not numbers-sensitive.
- **The psycholinguistic block adds essentially nothing over the structural block** for every layer/membership outcome: Kernel membership ΔAUC +0.00004; Core-vs-Satellite ΔAUC 0 (block 1 separates perfectly — Core is a deterministic SCC property); seed/MinSet membership ΔAUC +0.0004; layer index ΔR² +0.0066 (OLS) / pseudo-R² +0.0098 (Poisson). All well below the pre-registered 0.05 threshold → "the psycholinguistic signal *is* the structural signal." (`reports/psycholinguistic-regression-findings.md` §0, §3-4.)
- The Vincent-Lamarre "deeper layers = later-acquired" effect **vanishes** once you control for structure (standardized AoA coef on layer ≈ +0.009). (`reports/psycholinguistic-regression-findings.md` §3d, §5.)
- PF leg: reverse-PageRank incremental R² over `log(out-degree)` = +0.0093 (freq), +0.0023 (AoA), +0.0307 (concreteness — and that's a *suppression artifact*, partly proxying in-degree); FVS-seed membership adds +0.006 / +0.003 / +0.0001. Forward PageRank ≈ zero-correlated with all three norms. "Degree explains most of it" — confirmed. (`reports/psycholinguistic-regression-findings.md` §5.)
- **Verdict scope:** this kills the *strong* "large independent extra-graph (sensorimotor) residue" claim; it does **not** establish Yoneda-completeness, because of the binding confound — OEWN definitions are written by lexicographers *for human learners*, so concrete/early/frequent words become definers *because* readers are expected to know them, which *creates* the structural features. "Block 2 adds nothing over block 1" is equally consistent with "meaning is relational" and "the structure was built from psycholinguistic salience, so it screens it off." A clean test needs a causally-upstream sensorimotor norm (Lancaster sensorimotor / SER) used as an instrument, or a cross-dictionary comparison (vs Longman's controlled defining vocabulary). (`reports/psycholinguistic-regression-findings.md` §6.3, §7; `reports/swanson-synthesis.md` Yoneda section.)

### 1.4 The Swanson scoreboard (cross-domain links)
Per `reports/swanson-synthesis.md` §"Scoreboard" (and per-line `reports/swanson-*-findings.md`, `reports/codex-swanson-review.md`):
- **#5 Perron-Frobenius valuation — strongest.** Disjointness real (econ ↔ PF famously empty: Bidard-Erreygers-Parys "All but one"; Potron 1911 ignored ~90y). Naive form falsified; corrected form (reverse-PageRank) partly holds. Best outputs: a history-of-science "convergent rediscovery" paper, and "The Wrong Eigenvector" CL paper.
- **#3 Controllability / driver nodes — a divergence, not an identity.** FVS (cycle-breaking / well-foundedness) and structural-controllability driver nodes (Hopcroft-Karp maximum matching / dilation under linear-integrator dynamics) are genuinely different objects with predictably different selections — the *contrast* is the value. Numbers (`reports/maximum-matching-oewn.json`): full digraph 118,744 drivers (74.2%), Jaccard with the 2,370-node FVS seed = 0.012; Kernel subgraph 2,785 drivers (21.7%), only 255 in common (Jaccard 0.052); 266/288 Core nodes are drivers. Disagreement is structured (driver-not-grounder = morphological derivatives + numeral/proper-noun acyclic frontier; grounder-not-driver = abstract relational vocabulary). **The cleanest unexploited link is FVS-control in biology** (Mochizuki-Fiedler 2013 I & II; Zañudo-Yang-Albert PNAS 2017): that literature uses FVS for control of nonlinear dynamics and has no contact with dictionary-grounding, which also uses FVS — and the OEWN ~1.5% FVS-seed vs ~74% matching-driver split places the dictionary squarely in the biological-network regime (Zañudo 2016: 1–18% FVS vs 75–96% matching for gene-regulatory nets). (`reports/swanson-synthesis.md` §"Controllability"; `papers/index.md` entries for Fiedler 2013, Mochizuki 2013, Zañudo 2016, Gates 2016, Mochizuki 2010, Liu 2011.)
- **#2 Core-satellite ecology — falsified.** The bimodality falsifier fired: OEWN out-degree is log-series / species-abundance shaped (74.3% zeros + a monotone heavy tail), no second hump; Core (n=288) and Satellite (n=12,565) have near-identical out-degree distributions (means 43.8 vs 42.5). The lexical Core/Satellite split is *condensation topology* (source SCC vs downstream SCC inside the Kernel), not the common-vs-rare distinction that *is* Hanski's core/satellite. Only the weak diachronic form (colonization-extinction of the defining vocabulary across dictionary editions) survives, and it needs LDOCE-revision data; the strong rescue-effect prediction is contradicted by current data. Scripts `scripts/bimodality_check.py`, `scripts/bimodality_kernel.py`. (`reports/swanson-synthesis.md` §"Ecology".)
- **#1 Money / numéraire — demoted, not discarded.** Conflates 4 things (numéraire-as-gauge, gold-standard-as-convertibility, Harnad grounding, Sraffa standard commodity); subsumed by #5. The one tight bridge: Sraffa's standard commodity = the Perron eigenvector of the irreducible "basics" block of the input-output matrix, and Sraffa's basics/non-basics distinction is literally an SCC decomposition. Concrete result to fold into the valuation paper (the "weighted standard-MinSet theorem"): for an irreducible weighted Kernel `K` with PF vector `π` and any positive grounding-cost `g(v)`, set `c(v) = g(v)/π_v`; a `c`-minimum FVS is renormalization-invariant and is a Sraffa-style *standard MinSet* — canonical relative to `A_K` and `g`, not semantically ultimate. Two independent runs (in-repo + external deep-research) converged. Don't lead with the money framing. (`reports/swanson-synthesis.md` §"Money / numéraire"; `reports/swanson-money-numeraire-findings.md`, `...-deepresearch.md`.)
- **#4 Yoneda ↔ Harnad — compatible (Resolution A), not contradictory.** Yoneda determines an object *within a fixed category*; Harnad's point is the ambient category is not given a priori — it must be grounded into existence non-symbolically. MinSet = generating set for the category; MinSet non-uniqueness ≈ presentation non-uniqueness; Kernel/Core/Satellite ≈ graded grounding. Rider: a full structuralist owes mathematics the same grounding story (Benacerraf's access problem). The empirical leg has now been run (see §1.3) — and confirms Resolution A's framing without moving the philosophical needle (the lexicographers-write-for-learners confound is fatal to a stronger conclusion; **Yoneda-completeness is untouched**). (`reports/swanson-synthesis.md` §"Yoneda ↔ Harnad"; `reports/swanson-yoneda-harnad-findings.md`.)
- Codex's Predictions A (overlap-not-coincidence), B (Core/Satellite splits by spectral role), C (forward vs reverse exposes two senses of foundationalness) all confirmed; D only partly. (`reports/swanson-synthesis.md` §"one real result", `reports/codex-swanson-review.md`.)

### 1.5 The argumentation reframing (settled as a *framing*; demonstrators not yet built)
- The dictionary definition digraph = a **bipolar AF / ADF**: definition edges are *support* (`u → v` = "you can't know v until you know u"); the *attack* edges are exactly what the `lemma::pos` collapse discards (polysemy, prototype exceptions). Each word carries an acceptance condition over its definiens' statuses. (`reports/sibling-tools-connection.md` §1; `src/meanings/argumentation_bridge.py` docstring.)
- **MinSet = minimal-enforcement set** (`enforce_skeptical` in `argumentation/enforcement.py`): an FVS is "the minimal set of arguments whose status you must fix externally to make the verdict determinate" — Massé's theorem as a corollary of AF enforcement. **"Many MinSets, none privileged" = stable-extension multiplicity** (a 2-cycle `A↔B` has exactly two stable extensions `{A}`, `{B}`). (`reports/sibling-tools-connection.md` §2.)
- **Kernel-vs-loop-ecology = grounded-vs-preferred semantics** = foundationalism (Harnad) vs coherentism (Levary, distributional): Dung grounded = the foundationalist reading; preferred/stable = the coherentist readings. The Yoneda/Harnad debate gets a computational home. (`reports/sibling-tools-connection.md` §3.)
- Dictionary editions = AGM belief revision; foundational-ness = entrenchment / Spohn rank — *framework borrowed, library can't carry it* (`../belief-set` hard-caps `max_alphabet_size = 16`; build the OCF from the graph yourself, e.g. `layer index → Spohn rank`). NB: three unrelated "kernel"s — meanings-repo Kernel, AGM "kernel contraction" (Hansson), `af_revision.py`'s "kernel" (Baumann) — the word collision is a coincidence. (`reports/sibling-tools-connection.md` §4.)
- Polysemy / WSD = defeasible reasoning (`gunray` / ASPIC+): a circular ungrounded definition produces `UNDECIDED`, not an error; adding a grounding fact flips dependents to `YES` — a ~20-line working analogue of the whole grounding thesis. (`reports/sibling-tools-connection.md` §5.)
- Gradual / ranking-based semantics (`argumentation/ranking.py`: h-categorizer, burden, Potyka, DF-QuAD, Shapley) = "the spectral valuation done right" — a principled non-degenerate replacement for the PageRank attempt; caveat: may also collapse to degree on a sparse graph (a publishable negative result either way). (`reports/sibling-tools-connection.md` §6.)

### 1.6 The scaling result (argumentation semantics over the full 160k OEWN graph)
Per `reports/argumentation-bridge-oewn.md`, `reports/verify-argumentation-perf-fixes.md`:
- **Grounded extension is feasible at 160k in ~1.5 s** — via the *now-patched* `argumentation.dung.grounded_extension` (O(V+E) labelling reimplementation in `../argumentation` @ commit `4a1c31cc`, verified MERGE: 806 tests pass, 257 Dung + 120 bipolar instances bit-exact vs reference fixpoint, 50k-node graph in 0.13 s, |grounded| = 5043 on OEWN). The *old* implementation (commit `8d28624`) was super-quadratic in extension size and was killed after >200 s. `bipolar_grounded_extension` similarly fixed (compute the set-defeat closure once, delegate to `dung`). `meanings` pins to `4a1c31cc` (`pyproject.toml`; commit `26d2dd7`). NB: `reports/argumentation-bridge-oewn.md` still cites the pre-patch commit `8d28624` and the pre-patch >200 s timing — that report predates the upstream fix; the patched/pinned commit is `4a1c31cc`.
- The grounded extension is **not** the Kernel decomposition under either edge reading, and that's the expected answer: under the attack reading |grounded| = 5,043 (3.15%), 4,575 of them in the acyclic Rest, 468 inside the Kernel; `grounded ∪ Rest` = all nodes minus the 17,683 Kernel nodes left UNDEC. Under the support reading the defeat relation is empty, so grounded = all 160,010 nodes (analytic; a non-finding at scale). seed/MinSet and the grounded extension are essentially disjoint (overlap 2). (`reports/argumentation-bridge-oewn.md` §"Grounded extension".)
- **The Kernel AF has NO stable extension — UNSAT.** z3 (`argumentation.af_sat.find_stable_extension`) decides the whole 18,151-node Kernel AF as UNSAT in 8.1 s, and its largest SCC (8,138 nodes) as UNSAT in 3.3 s; of ~693 non-singleton Kernel SCCs, 630 SAT, 63 UNSAT (odd cycles). So "MinSet ↔ stable-extension outsiders" does **not** hold here. (`reports/argumentation-bridge-oewn.md` §"Stable-extension probe".)
- **Nothing is blocked on a hard SCC**: z3 decides the 8,138-node giant core in seconds; the blockers were all *library implementation* (now fixed for grounded). The natural next module is the SCC + FVS-backdoor + z3 divide-and-conquer front-end. (`reports/argumentation-bridge-oewn.md` §"Verdict", §"Next module".)
