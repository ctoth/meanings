# Citations

## Reference List

Bond, F., & Foster, R. (2013). Linking and extending an open multilingual WordNet.

Miller, G. A. (1995). WordNet: A lexical database for English.

Navigli, R., & Ponzetto, S. P. (2012). BabelNet: The automatic construction, evaluation and application of a wide-coverage multilingual semantic network.

Speer, R., Chin, J., & Havasi, C. (2017). ConceptNet 5.5: An open multilingual graph of general knowledge.

Bosselut, A., Rashkin, H., Sap, M., Malaviya, C., Celikyilmaz, A., & Choi, Y. (2019). COMET: Commonsense transformers for automatic knowledge graph construction.

Hubert, N., Perron, M., Aquin, D., Monticolo, D., & Bruneau, A. (2024). PyGraf: Configurable generation of synthetic schemas and knowledge graphs.

Text2KGBench (2023), Text2KG (2024), and related ontology-driven generation benchmarks and systems.

GraphRAG (2024), knowledge-distillation and hybrid symbolic-neural graph systems.

WordNet-based model systems, COLAKE, KEPLER, and related hybrid lexical/knowledge models.

## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **WordNet / Open English WordNet**: the primary manually curated baseline OpenGloss compares itself against.
- **BabelNet** and **ConceptNet**: useful comparison resources for coverage, multilinguality, and graph design.
- **PyGraf**, **COMET**, and **GraphRAG**: adjacent structured-generation and graph-construction tools.
- **COLAKE** and **KEPLER**: hybrid symbolic/neural approaches relevant if we later align lexical graphs with learned representations.
