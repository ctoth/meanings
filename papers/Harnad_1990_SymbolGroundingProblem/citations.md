## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Newell (1980), "Physical symbol systems"**: central symbolic-AI counterpart to Harnad's critique.
- **Fodor and Pylyshyn (1988), "Connectionism and cognitive architecture"**: key debate about systematicity and compositionality.
- **Gibson (1979), *An ecological approach to visual perception***: source for invariants and perception-grounded category learning.
- **Searle (1980), "Minds, brains and programs"**: Chinese Room argument that Harnad retools into a dictionary-based regress.
- **Kripke (1980), *Naming and necessity***: relevant for reference and naming once grounded symbols enter a lexicon.
