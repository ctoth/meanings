# Abstract

## Original Text (Verbatim)

This paper has no modern standalone abstract. Its opening sections argue that machine translation is “theoretically impossible” in its current form because it lacks a semantic discipline capable of detecting message-connection rather than merely syntactic connection, and then propose a semantic interlingua built from a small set of minimals, connectives, and tree/lattice operations. *(p.438-441)*

---

## Our Interpretation

This is premodern computational semantics, but not empty speculation. Masterman is explicitly trying to build a minimal semantic stock and a formal compositional system over it, which makes the paper a real historical ancestor of later work on defining vocabularies, semantic primitives, and recursive lexical bases.
