## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Richens (1956/1958)**: direct interlingua lineage.
- **Masterman and Kay (1960)**: immediate precursor on mechanical pidgin translation.
- **Needham / Parker-Rhodes / Sparck Jones**: the early Cambridge/CLRU semantic-and-retrieval cluster around this work.
- **Koenig (1936)**: graph-theoretic ancestry for some of the tree/lattice machinery.
