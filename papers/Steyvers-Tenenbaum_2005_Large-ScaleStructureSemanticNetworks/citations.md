## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Barabási and Albert (1999)** and **Watts and Strogatz (1998)**: the two core network-science baselines behind the paper’s structural claims.
- **Landauer and Dumais (1997)**: the distributed-semantic alternative the authors explicitly test and partially reject as a fit for the observed graph statistics.
- **Zipf (1965)**: important older bridge between lexical regularity and large-scale power-law structure.
- **Milgram (1967)** and **Kleinberg (2000)**: relevant if we care about semantic search and navigability rather than only static graph structure.
