## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Chicoisne et al. (2008), "Grounding Abstract Word Definitions in Prior Concrete Experience"**: direct adjacent work on extending grounding from concrete to abstract words.
- **Clark (2003), "Recursion Through Dictionary Definition Space"**: likely an early graph treatment of dictionary definitional space.
- **Tarjan (1972)** and **Karp (1972)**: the exact graph-algorithm backbone for SCCs and feedback vertex sets.
- **Procter (1978, 1995)** and **Fellbaum (1998)**: the concrete lexicographic resources proposed for real extraction.
- **Steyvers and Tenenbaum (2005)**: relevant comparison point for semantic-network structure outside dictionary definitions.
