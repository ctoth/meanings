## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Lin and Jou (2000)**: directed feedback vertex set algorithms, closer to dictionary `MinSet` computation than this undirected paper.
- **Schwikowski and Speckenmeyer (2002)**: direct antecedent for enumerating all minimal solutions of feedback problems.
- **Festa et al. (1999)**: broad survey of feedback-set methods and formulations.
- **Lapointe et al. (2012)**: not cited here, but the obvious in-collection successor for generating many minimal directed seeds.
