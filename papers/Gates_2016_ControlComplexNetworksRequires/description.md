This paper uses exhaustively enumerated Boolean-network ensembles and three Boolean models of biochemical regulation (Drosophila segment polarity, yeast cell cycle, Arabidopsis floral organ) to show that structure-only controllability methods — structural controllability (maximum matching) and minimum dominating set — fail once dynamics are introduced, both under- and over-estimating the number of driver variables and picking the wrong ones. The decisive factor is how canalizing the automata transition functions are: most structural edges are functionally redundant, so the effective structure graph diverges from the wiring diagram, and only when canalization collapses the effective structure to a linear chain do the structure-only methods become correct. It is the methodological caution behind this project's OEWN definition-digraph finding that maximum-matching driver nodes pick the wrong words for lexical grounding while the dynamics-agnostic feedback-vertex-set framing (Massé/Vincent-Lamarre/Picard plus Fiedler/Mochizuki/Zañudo) is the one that transfers, precisely because FVS-control is a worst-case-over-all-dynamics guarantee.
