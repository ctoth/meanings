## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **20. Liu, Slotine & Barabási, "Controllability of complex networks", Nature 473 (2011)** — the structural-controllability / maximum-matching method this paper rebuts; the direct counterpoint to the repo's FVS framing. (Check whether a `papers/Liu_2011_*` dir exists; if so, cross-link.)
- **21/22. Nacher & Akutsu, MDS controllability** — the minimum-dominating-set method, the second structure-only target.
- **30. Cowan et al., "Nodal Dynamics, Not Degree Distributions, ...", PLoS ONE 7 (2012)** — independent earlier critique of SC on the same grounds (dynamics matter); good companion lead.
- **31. Sun & Motter, "Controllability Transition and Nonlocality in Network Control", PRL 110 (2013)** — nonlocality argument against structure-only control.
- **7. Marques-Pita & Rocha, "Canalization and control in automata networks", PLoS ONE 8 (2013)** — the effective-connectivity / schema-redescription machinery underpinning the canalization argument; Drosophila segmentation case.
- **60. Zañudo & Albert (2015)** — already in the collection as `Zañudo_2016_Structure-basedControlComplexNetworks`; stabilization-subgraph / FVS-style control.
- **52. Ingram, Stumpf & Stark, "Network motifs: structure does not determine function", BMC Genomics 7 (2006)** — the motif-level antecedent of the same lesson.
