## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Refs 3 & 21 — Fiedler, Mochizuki, Kurosawa, Saito (2013), "Dynamics and control at feedback vertex sets" I & II (J Dyn Diff Eqns 25(3):563-604; J Theor Biol 335:130-146):** the FC theorems this whole paper rests on — overriding the FVS drives a nonlinear-dynamic network to a chosen attractor regardless of functional form. The formal foundation for treating a lexical grounding set as a feedback vertex set.
- **Ref 1 — Liu, Slotine, Barabási (2011), "Controllability of complex networks," Nature 473:167-173:** the structural-controllability / maximum-matching method this paper compares against — the "74% drivers" side of the OEWN comparison.
- **Ref 6 — Zañudo, Albert (2015), "Cell fate reprogramming by control of intracellular network dynamics," PLoS Comput Biol 11(4):e1004193:** shows a *subset* of the FVS (via stable motifs) can suffice in Boolean models — directly relevant to whether a sub-FVS lexical seed works.
- **Ref 7/8 — Gates, Rocha (2016), "Control of complex networks requires both structure and dynamics," Sci Rep 6:24456:** the structure-vs-dynamics caveat; useful counterweight.
- **Ref 18 — Liu, Barabási (2016), "Control principles of complex systems," Rev Mod Phys 88:035006:** comprehensive survey tying SC, FC, and other methods together.
