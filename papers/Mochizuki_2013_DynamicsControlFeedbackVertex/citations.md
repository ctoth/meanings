## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Fiedler, Mochizuki, Kurosawa, Saito (2013), "Dynamics and control at feedback vertex sets I"** — the direct companion; the control theorem (clamping the FVS steers the global attractor). Read together with this paper.
- **Fomin et al. (2008), Minimum Feedback Vertex Set Problem** — already in this collection; the algorithmic side (computing FVS, which is NP-hard).
- **Liu, Slotine, Barabási (2011), Controllability of Complex Networks** (Nature) — the structural-controllability alternative; needed to understand why FVS (graph-combinatorial, nonlinear) vs driver-node (Jacobian-rank, linear) differ.
- **Joly (2012)** — precise mathematical version of "which sensors determine the dynamics" for ODE networks; the rigorous backbone for the determining-nodes claim.
- **Mochizuki et al. (2010)** — earlier FVS-and-dynamics paper this one extends; foundational for the "determining nodes" concept.
