Introduces "linkage logic" — a method to determine the diversity of steady states of an ODE regulatory network using only the wiring (which node regulates which), not the signs or strengths of regulation.
Its central object is the set of "informative nodes," determined purely from the in-link structure, whose activities at a steady state determine every other node's activity for any admissible nonlinearity; a "Principle of Compatibility" bounds steady-state diversity by the number of informative nodes and a "Principle of Dependency" gives a wiring-consistency check on observed steady-state sets, illustrated on the ascidian Ciona developmental gene network.
This is the origin paper for "informative nodes" — the steady-state precursor of the feedback-vertex-set characterization (Fiedler–Mochizuki 2013); for a definition digraph the informative nodes are the minimal lexical grounding set, computable from the wiring alone.
