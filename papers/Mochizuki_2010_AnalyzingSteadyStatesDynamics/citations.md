## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Imai, Levine, Satoh, Satou (2006), Science 312:1183–1187** — the experimentally determined ascidian *Ciona* gene regulatory network the paper's main application is built on; needed to check the "78 unobserved steady states" prediction.
- **Mochizuki, A. (2008), J. Theor. Biol. 250:307–321** — "Structure of regulatory networks and diversity of gene expression patterns": the binary-steady-state precursor this paper generalizes; the direct intellectual predecessor.
- **Mochizuki, A. (2005), J. Theor. Biol. 236:291–310** — "An analytical study of the number of steady states in gene regulatory networks": RSP/SSP background.
- **Snoussi & Thomas (1993); Thomas, Thieffry, Kaufman (1995)** — feedback-loop-characteristic-state framework; the closest prior structure↔dynamics theory the linkage logic improves on.
- **Shen-Orr et al. (2002); Mangan & Alon (2003)** — network-motif line of work the paper contrasts itself against (substructure vs whole-system).
