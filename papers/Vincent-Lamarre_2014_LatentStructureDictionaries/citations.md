## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Harnad (1990), "The Symbol Grounding Problem"**: philosophical grounding constraint for any graph-only kernel account.
- **Blondin Masse et al. (2008), "How Is Meaning Grounded in Dictionary Definitions?"**: likely the immediate precursor to the current paper's dictionary-grounding line.
- **Picard et al. (2013), "Hidden Structure and Function in the Lexicon"**: appears to bridge dictionary game data and latent graph structure.
- **Fomin et al. (2008)** and **Festa et al. (1999)**: core algorithmic references for the feedback vertex set / MinSet problem.
- **Steyvers and Tenenbaum (2005)**: comparison target between definitional graphs and broader semantic/associative networks.
