## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Leavy et al. (2012), "Loops and self-reference in the construction of dictionaries"**: very likely relevant to cycle structure in definitional graphs.
- **Lapointe et al. (2012), "Enumerating minimum feedback vertex sets in directed graphs"**: useful if we want multiple kernel seeds rather than just one.
- **Blondin-Massé et al. (2013), "Symbol Grounding and the Origin of Language: From Show to Tell"**: extends the grounding story beyond dictionaries.
- **Lin and Jou (2000)** and **Nemhauser and Wolsey (1999)**: algorithmic references for MGS/FVS computation.
- **Wilson (1987)**: source of the psycholinguistic variables used to assign function to hidden graph structure.
